## Project Overview

### ChEMBL Database
The [ChEMBL Database](https://www.google.com/url?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ebi.ac.uk%2Fchembl%2F) is a large, open-access database of chemical molecules and their biological effects. Maintained by the European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI), it holds data for over 2.2 million compounds extracted from scientific studies, patents, and direct submissions to help scientists find new medicines.
- **Compound details:** Chemical structures, names, and physical traits.
- **Bioactivity data:** How small molecules act on proteins, cells, and whole living bodies (such as binding or functional results)
- **ADMET data:** Details on absorption, distribution, metabolism, excertion, and toxicity
- **Drug pipeline info:** Details on approved medicines and drugs tested in clinical trials
